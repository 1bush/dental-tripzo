FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything patients ask before booking.

Are the materials really EU-certified?

Yes — Straumann implants and Ivoclar E-max veneers with traceable serial numbers, documented in your warranty certificate.

What is included in the package price?

Treatment, 5★ hotel with breakfast, all VIP transfers, translator and aftercare. Only flights (€39+) and food outside the hotel are extra.

How long do I need to stay?

5 days for veneers and crowns. Implants need one 5-day visit, then a second short visit after 3 months for final crowns.

What if something goes wrong at home?

You are covered by a 10-year international warranty, free video check-ups and partner dentists in Poland, Czechia and Slovakia.
